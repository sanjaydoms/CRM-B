03Body measurements

Step 2 of the order wizard

Seven standard measurements, plus room for anything garment-specific. Every time a client's measurements are changed, the previous version is kept automatically — there's a full history, not just the current numbers.

FieldNotes
BustAll optional individually, but the intake step expects at least the ones relevant to the garment type. Recorded to two decimal places.
Waist
Hips
Shoulder
Arm length
Neck
Length
Additional measurementsOpen-ended — for parts specific to a garment (e.g. sleeve circumference on a fitted blouse) that don't fit the seven standard fields
Why this matters downstream

Measurements are checked, not just stored: an order can't move to "assigned to tailor" until measurements exist, and Style DNA's size estimate reads directly from the bust figure.

04Design preferences

FieldNotes
NotesOptionalFree text describing the design

SourceRequiredBoutique catalogue, Custom design, Previous design, Pinterest, Google images, Customer sketch, or Designer sketch — defaults to Boutique catalogue
Reference linksOptionalExternal inspiration URLs, e.g. a Pinterest board

A design can later be marked approved for production by staff, with one reference image chosen as the final look — that's a staff action, not a customer input, so it's covered in the features note rather than here.

07What we don't ask for

Worth stating plainly, since it shapes what the client-facing side of the product can ever show them:

  • The customer never logs in and never fills in any of these fields themselves — every input above is entered by boutique staff, on the customer's behalf, during a conversation or fitting
  • No consent or communication-preference capture beyond the single "preferred communication channel" field
  • No payment details are collected or stored — payment status is recorded, not processed
