What it is

What is Scaleezy Boutique?

Scaleezy Boutique is CRM and order-management software for custom-tailoring businesses — bridal boutiques, saree houses, blouse specialists and designer ateliers that cut and stitch every piece to a specific client's measurements.

It replaces the measurement notebook, the order register and the WhatsApp thread with one record per client: their measurements, their design references, every order they have placed, which of your masters is working on it right now, what is still owed, and where the garment has reached on the floor.

Unlike generic retail POS or off-the-shelf CRM, it is built around the way a made-to-order garment actually moves — eight stages from Received to Delivered, nine specialist roles from Measurement Master to QC Master, and a costing sheet that separates fabric, embroidery, customisation and tailoring charges.

The problem

The order is in three places, and none of them agree.

A register at the counter. A measurement book with the cutting master. A WhatsApp thread with the customer. When the bride calls to ask if her blouse is ready, someone has to walk to the back and find out.

  • Measurements on paper
  • Orders in a register
  • Status only the tailor knows
  • Trial dates missed
  • Balance payments forgotten
  • Fabric counted by memory
  • "Is it ready?" — five times a day

Your floor, your roles

Software that knows a Maggam Master from a Cutting Master.

Generic CRM gives you "staff". A tailoring floor does not work that way — each stage only accepts the specialists who can actually do it. A one-person boutique keeps using Master for everything; a larger studio splits the work.

For your customer

She can check on her own lehenga.

Every order gets a signed tracking link. No app, no account, no password — she opens it and sees exactly where her garment has reached. The calls stop because the answer is already in her hand.

When the garment is finished you photograph it from every angle — front, back, sleeve, dupatta, close-up detail — and publish the gallery deliberately, once. A half-uploaded gallery is not what you want her looking at.

Status messages queue up for you to send from your own WhatsApp number, so the boutique she hears from is still the boutique she knows.

The money

Know what a garment earned, not just what it sold for.

Fabric, embroidery, customisation and tailoring are costed as separate lines, because they are separate decisions. When a client asks why a blouse costs what it costs, the breakdown is already there.

Advances and balances sit against the order, so the outstanding list is a query rather than a memory test.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is Scaleezy Boutique only for custom tailoring?

It is built for made-to-order garments — bridal wear, sarees and blouses, lehengas, sherwanis and bespoke menswear. If you sell finished stock off a rack and never take a measurement, a standard retail POS will fit you better. If you do both, the boutique product handles the made-to-order side and the wider Scaleezy platform covers retail.

Do my tailors need to use a computer?

No. Staff work from their phone — the floor screens are built mobile-first, with a task queue showing only what is assigned to that person. The owner uses the full dashboard; the cutting master just needs to see the next job and mark it done.

Can my customer track her order without installing anything?

Yes. Each order has a signed tracking link that works in any browser with no login and no app. The link carries both the boutique and the order, so nothing else is exposed. You control when finished-garment photographs become visible on it.

Does it send WhatsApp messages automatically?

Messages are prepared and queued against the order, and you send them from your own WhatsApp number. That is deliberate — a bride should hear from her boutique, not from an unknown gateway number. Each message tracks as queued, sent, delivered, read or failed.

How is my boutique's data kept separate from other boutiques?

Through schema-based multi-tenancy in PostgreSQL. Each boutique's records live in their own database schema rather than sharing tables with a tenant ID column, so separation is enforced by the database itself.

Can I move my existing customers and measurements in?

Yes — existing client records, measurements and order history can be imported when your workspace is set up. Ask about migration during the demo and bring whatever you have, including a spreadsheet or a photographed register.

How long does it take to get running?

A workspace is created for you with your staff roles, garment types and stage list configured to how your floor already works. Most of the setup effort is agreeing the stages and the costing lines, not the software.
