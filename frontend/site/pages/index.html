What it is

What is Scaleezy Boutique?

Scaleezy Boutique is CRM and order-management software for custom-tailoring businesses — bridal boutiques, saree houses, blouse specialists and designer ateliers that cut and stitch every piece to a specific client's measurements.

It replaces the measurement notebook, the order register and the WhatsApp thread with one record per client: their measurements, their design references, every order they have placed, which of your masters is working on it right now, what is still owed, and where the garment has reached on the floor.

Unlike generic retail POS or off-the-shelf CRM, it is built around the way a made-to-order garment actually moves — eight stages from Received to Delivered, nine specialist roles from Measurement Master to QC Master, and a costing sheet that separates fabric, embroidery, customisation and tailoring charges.

The problem

The order is in three places, and none of them agree.

A register at the counter. A measurement book with the cutting master. A WhatsApp thread with the customer. When the bride calls to ask if her blouse is ready, someone has to walk to the back and find out.

  • Measurements on paper
  • Orders in a register
  • Status only the tailor knows
  • Trial dates missed
  • Balance payments forgotten
  • Fabric counted by memory
  • "Is it ready?" — five times a day

The flow

Eight stages, from counter to doorstep.

Every order carries its own stage list. Each stage has an owner, a start time, a completion time and an SLA — so a delay is visible on the day it happens, not at delivery.

1

Received

Client, garment and measurements captured at the counter.

2

Confirmed

Design agreed, costing set, advance recorded.

3

Stylist review

Design references and fabric checked before cutting.

4

Design & creation

Pattern, cutting, embroidery and stitching on the floor.

5

Quality check

QC master inspects against the measurement ledger.

6

Ready for dispatch

Pressed, packed, photographed, balance settled.

7

Shipped

Courier and tracking number, or counter pickup.

8

Delivered

Closed, with the full history kept against the client.
