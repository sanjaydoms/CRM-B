What it is

What is Scaleezy Boutique?

CRM and order-management software for businesses that make every garment to a specific person's measurements.

Scaleezy Boutique is CRM and order-management software for custom-tailoring businesses — bridal boutiques, saree houses, blouse specialists and designer ateliers that cut and stitch every piece to a specific client's measurements.

It replaces the measurement notebook, the order register and the WhatsApp thread with one record per client: their measurements, their design references, every order they have placed, which of your masters is working on it right now, what is still owed, and where the garment has reached on the floor.

Who it is for

Built for made-to-order, not off-the-rack.

The distinction that matters is not what you sell — it is whether a garment is cut to one person's body. If it is, everything downstream changes: the record you keep, the people who touch it, and how long it takes.

Bridal boutiques

Long journeys, multiple fittings, alterations, and a date that cannot move. The order history matters as much as the order.

Saree houses & blouse specialists

High volume, small garments, deep variant complexity, and measurements that get reused for years.

Lehenga & ethnic wear studios

Embroidery runs that sit between cutting and stitching and dominate the timeline.

Designer ateliers

Collections, design approvals and a named designer behind each piece.

Bespoke menswear

Pattern drafting, repeat clients, and fit records that must survive staff turnover.

Boutiques with a floor

Anywhere more than one person touches a garment and the handoffs are currently verbal.

How it differs

Why retail software does not fit a tailoring floor.

Most software assumes a finished product moves from shelf to customer. A made-to-order garment does not exist yet when the order is placed, which breaks nearly every assumption underneath a POS or a generic CRM.

What it is not.

Software pages usually skip this part. Knowing where a tool stops is how you avoid buying the wrong one.

Not a retail POS

If most of your revenue is finished stock sold off a rack, a proper POS will serve you better. Run both if you do both.

Not an accounting package

It records what an order cost and what was paid against it. It is not your books and does not file your returns.

Not a body-scanning app

Measurements are taken by your Measurement Master the way they always were. This is the ledger that keeps them, not a device that takes them.

Not an e-commerce storefront

There is no public catalogue with a checkout. The customer-facing surface is order tracking for work already commissioned.

Not a WhatsApp bot

Messages are prepared and queued. You send them from your own number, deliberately — see for customers.

Not a replacement for your masters

It records and routes the work. The craft, and the judgement about the craft, stays with the people who have it.
