Worth bringing

Three things that make it useful.

One live order

Ideally a complicated one. Simple orders make any software look good; the bridal piece with three fittings is the real test.

Your stage names

However you already describe the steps on your floor. The stage list gets configured to match your words, not ours.

Your costing sheet

However you currently price a garment — a printed sheet or a page in a register is fine. That is what the costing lines get mapped to.
