## Common Materials Used in Bridal Maggam Work

The most frequently used materials for premium bridal blouses and sarees include:

- Silk fabric
- Velvet
- Resham thread
- Cotton thread
- Gold & silver zari
- Dabka
- Nakshi
- Kasab
- Salma
- Sitara
- Seed beads (11/0, 10/0, 8/0)
- Bugle beads
- Kundan stones
- Rhinestones
- Swarovski crystals
- Half pearls
- Full pearls
- Sequins (3 mm, 5 mm, 7 mm)
- Mirror pieces
- Gota Patti
- Pearl chains
- Stone chains
- Metal studs
- Decorative laces
- Tassels (Latkans)
- Aari needles
- Maggam frame (Adda)
- Tracing paper
- Carbon paper
- Fabric glue
- Buckram/interlining
- Embroidery scissors
- Measuring tape
- Thread wax

This list covers nearly every category of materials currently used in traditional Indian Maggam, Aari, Zardosi, and contemporary couture hand embroidery — intended for use by a professional embroidery workshop or manufacturing unit.
