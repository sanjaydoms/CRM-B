## Complete Workflow (Apparel Lifecycle)

1. Market research & collection planning
2. Design & tech pack creation
3. Fabric and trim sourcing
4. Pattern making & grading
5. Sample development
6. Fit approval
7. Fabric inspection
8. Fabric spreading & cutting
9. Embroidery, printing, and embellishment
10. Sewing & assembly
11. Finishing & pressing
12. Quality control
13. Branding (labels, tags)
14. Packaging
15. Inventory & warehousing
16. Retail and/or e-commerce listing
17. Order processing
18. Shipping & delivery
19. Returns & alterations
20. Customer support & repeat sales

This checklist represents the core components of a professional apparel brand, whether launching a boutique or scaling to a full garment manufacturing operation serving retail, wholesale, exports, or D2C.
